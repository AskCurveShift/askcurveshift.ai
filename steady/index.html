Steady

iPhone training companion

Training, one day at a time.

Steady brings push-up training, runs, lifting sessions, and recovery context into one daily agenda. It shows weekly progress and helps you prepare for longer-term endurance goals.

Optional Lose It! report import

If you choose to connect Google, Steady requests read-only Gmail access to find Lose It! Daily Report emails. It reads matching reports, extracts nutrition details, and saves those details on your iPhone. Steady does not send, change, or delete your email.

Google's permission can read your mailbox, although Steady only searches for recent Lose It! reports. You can disconnect Google and delete imported reports in the app.

Built around your data

Steady can read workouts, nutrition, weight, and Apple Watch metrics that you allow through Apple Health. Optional provider connections show available iFit and Tonal plans. AI coaching uses summaries only when you turn on sharing in Steady.
